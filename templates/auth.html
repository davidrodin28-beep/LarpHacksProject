WHAT IT TRACKS

Practice estimates, sport by sport

  • BoxingJab, cross, and 1–2 sequences; arm extension and chin position.
  • BasketballShot-like arm releases and estimated jump lift.
  • Racket sportsRacket-swing-like wrist movement and rough direction.
  • ArcheryDraw-like arm position and steady hold duration.
  • Volleyball & baseballOverhead arm extension and jump estimates where available.
  • Soccer, running & golfKick-like leg extension, alternating knee lifts, or broad swing movement.

Choose a 10, 20, or 30 second practice round for a camera-based score and a couple of movement cues. These are rough practice estimates, not professional coaching or validated measurements.

◎Privacy by design
Camera frames are analyzed in your browser and are not uploaded or recorded. An account is used to access the tracker; movement results are not saved to your account.
